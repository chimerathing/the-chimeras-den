I'm a digital and traditional artist who's been drawing seriously ever since the 3rd grade. I primarily enjoy drawing fanart of my favorite characters and media, especially the queer ones. This page is an archive of pretty much (almost) everything I've ever drawn.

Most of my art is not publicly available on the internet for various reasons, so this is the only place where you'll be able to view all of it. The only art I've excluded is stuff that I've deemed to be low-effort, that was drawn for people I no longer associate with, or that I genuinely just could not find. Enjoy!

All of my art is free for any NON-COMMERCIAL use with credit (unless it's a commissioned piece). I DO NOT consent to any of my work being used to train AI, or for anything related to crypto/NFT nonsense.

Software: IbisPaint X & Magma (primarily), Clip Studio Paint & Krita (on rare and special occasions)

Devices: iPhone 14, Wacom One 13
