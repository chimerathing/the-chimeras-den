We're collectively transmasc and nonbinary/multigendered, and if you're referring to us as a collective, or no particular alter is shown to be fronting, please call us Gabriel and use he/him pronouns! Otherwise, please refer to us by the current main fronter's name. This site is primarily run by the host Gabriel, but we thought it'd be fun to let our headmates have their own personal pages to mess around with HTML/CSS on. We're very introject heavy (mainly fictives), and we prefer being treated as parts of a whole rather than as entirely seperate beings from eachother. We are multiple people, but we function mostly as a unit.

We do not believe that endogenics or non-traumagenic/non-disordered systems in general are real thing and would prefer if people who believe themselves to be one of those did not engage with us, as we find the concept to be extremely ableist and generally just don't feel comfortable or safe around people in that community. We don't wanna get involved in discourse, just be respectful of our boundaries. Thank you!

If you have any questions regarding our disorder, don't be afraid to ask! While we may not know everything and definitely cannot speak for ALL systems, we know a good amount of stuff about CDDs and are always open to educating others and talking about our personal experiences.
