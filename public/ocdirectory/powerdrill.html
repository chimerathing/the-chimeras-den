Power Drill

Name: Power Drill (nicknamed "Drilly" by Lightbulb)
Gender: Transmasc & Pangender
Pronouns: he/xe/it
Orientation: Lesbian
Species: Red & black electric power drill
Status: Alive

Power Drill is a rejected contestant who never got the chance to compete on Inanimate Insanity during it's original runtime, having managed to escape MeAfterlife alongside Guitar some time before Teddy Bear, Spikey, Tapey, and Jack did so themselves by beating the basketball minigame. Lacking a lot of social skills as well as understanding of himself and his own emotions, he's incredibly socially awkward and tries a bit too hard to appear friendly to others, and is cursed with a constant fear of rejection and being viewed as "weird" or any other negative thing by the people around him. This causes him to be quite the pushover. He only finds himself being able to let loose when he's with people he feels comfortable around, but oftentimes will become avoidant towards them if he suspects that he's overstayed his welcome. He can sometimes be a bit temperamental, but tries his best to keep those feelings of rage shoved down. As the cherry on top, he also deals with severe jealousy issues.
