Personality

Megaphone is a relatively friendly and extroverted person overall, and tends to have an “i don’t give a fuck” type of attitude towards a majority of things. With his friends, or people she otherwise feels comfortable around, she can be pretty flirtatious, but also engages in a bit of pissing them off for fun. She’s pretty confident in herself and has a very strong sense of identity, being very open about the kind of person he is, especially in terms of his queerness. He doesn’t take kindly to people trying to control or change him in any regards, she just wants to be allowed to be herself and for others to fuck off if they take issue with it. He enjoys being able to just let loose and enjoy life, not letting himself dwell on or stress over negative things too much. When things do get under his skin though, the result isn’t very pretty. He can become rather snippy and hostile with people, and enters an irrational state of mind where he exhibits a lot of self-destructive behaviors. He also thinks lowly of people that she perceives as not being fully understanding of him or his issues. She doesn’t particularly enjoy this aspect of herself at all, but a lot of unresolved trauma throughout her life has brought her to a point where he isn’t entirely sure of how to regulate himself or his emotions, and he finds it better for him to just ignore it all as confronting it would bring him significant stress that he has yet to feel ready to deal with. That doesn't stop him from complaining about how shitty some parts of his life are, though, he just feels way too helpless to truly do anything about it.

He's constantly paranoid of people using him for his fame, so she tries to be careful of who exactly she decides to be friends with. She has been faced with a lot of people throughout her life that wanted to take advantage of him to due the fact that he's famous, and as well as attractive, and as a result generally tends to be pretty skeptical of people despite his usual laid-back nature. He also has been frequently objectified and dehumanized for most of her life and career, especially for being trans and intersex. Overall, he hates the fact that he is famous because of this. He enjoys doing what he does and is happy with where he's at in life for the most part, but he actually doesn't really enjoy the fame that has come from it at all. He doesn't like having so many eyes on him. It feels violating. He just wants people in his life that will treat him like a normal person, not a celebrity or sexual object.
