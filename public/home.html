WELCOME!

Hello there, and welcome! It seems you've stumbled upon my personal website. My name is Gabriel, but you can also call me Painty, Falin, or Teddie. I'm an artist who has spent most of his life in front of a computer screen and likes vocal synths and object shows a little bit TOO much, hence why this place is themed the way it is. This site serves as a way for me to express myself freely without fear of judgement, as well as an outlet for my art and various other things that are important to me. It's my safe haven away from the eyes rest of the internet, and also is where you can find a majority of the things I've created in one place. It's a permanent work in progress so expect things to change and new things to be added overtime! That being said, enjoy exploring!

My site is rated 16+ for mature themes/subject matter, as well as some possible suggestive content and cartoon gore, but there will never be any explicit sexual imagery hosted on this site. All sensitive content is marked with a warning beforehand, so you may browse in peace!

Wanna get to know me a bit more? Well then, click here! And while you're on your way out, maybe sign my guestbook?

This site was coded in Zen Browser at a 1920 x 1080 screen resolution, and has been tested on Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. I'm aiming for it to be as accessible as possible while still maintaining a clear artistic vision. If anything looks weird or broken, please let me know! I tend to make a lot of mistakes when coding and I don't always notice, so I appreciate any help or feedback.
