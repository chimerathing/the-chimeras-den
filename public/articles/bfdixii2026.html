MY EXPERIENCE AT THE BFDI X II 2026 MEETUP/TOUR


I've been meaning to make a post about what going to the meetup was like since I got home from it on July 11th but I've felt way too demotivated to do much on my website so I haven't gotten around to it. I don't have much else to do at the moment though and since the first half of the meetups are over now (at least i think they are? I don't know I haven't been paying much attention to stuff about the meetups since the one I went to has been done) I might as well relay some of my thoughts about it!


II's 15th anniversary screening back at the beginning of april this year was nothing short of fucking awesome, but the BFDI x II meetup was genuinely was the biggest highlight of my year. This year was my very first time going to any of the meetups. I missed out on a chance to go last year in 2025 due to my mom unfortunately deciding to wait too damn long to buy tickets, so I was absolutely dead set on not missing this time around. Inanimate Insanity has been my biggest comfort show since the beginning of last year, and my life has changed drastically for the better in so many ways ever since then, so I was really REALLY looking forward to this event especially since the episodes they were showing this time around were the biggest ones of the show's latest season yet, and oh fucking boy I seriously do not regret going one bit. I genuinely will never forget how I felt watching that episode in a huge theater full of thousands of other people and bawling my eyes the FUCK out because of just how emotional it made me. It's been a long while since I've seriously cried over any piece of media, and out of all of the things it could've been it was the show about neurodivergent and gay ass sentient objects competeing on a reality show. WHEN I TELL YOU I WAILED LIKE A FUCKING TODDLER AT MY WHOLE ASS 17 COMING ON 18 YEARS OF AGE I FUCKING MEAN IT. I'M A GROWN ASS MAN AND THE SHOW WITH THE FUCKING PHONE THAT EATS COOKIES MADE ME CRY. AND NOT ONLY WERE THEY SAD TEARS BECAUSE OF WHAT HAPPENED IN THE EPISODE BUT THEY WERE ALSO TEARS OF PURE JOY BECAUSE GOD FUCKING DAMMIT I LOVE THIS STUPID FUCKING OBJECT SHOW SO MUCH!!!!!!!! WHAT THE FUCK IS MY LIFE!!!!!!!! Oh yeah I was honestly really only there for Inanimate Insanity because I'm not really a huge BFDI fan, it's not really my cup of tea as oppossed to II but I did mildly enjoy the episode they showed for that as well lol. II is a lot more appealing to me in like every possible way especially in terms of themes and overrall writing quality, also the cast in II is just a lot more compelling to me for many reasons, but I can still appreciate BFDI for what it is and how much it's inspired and brought joy a significant amount of people just as II has done for me :]


I specificially went to the July 11th meetup in NYC at 11 AM and it was just a really great experience overall. All the people there were really cool and there were SOOO many cosplayers. I went in a cosplay of my Paintbrush gijinka and I'm honestly surprised anyone even recognized what I was cosplaying at all cuz it was a really cheap outfit thrown together with mostly shit I already had in my wardrobe and I wasn't even wearing a wig or anything LOL. I slightly considered wearing one but the only one I have was originally for my Falin Touden cosplay that I wore to Anime NYC last year and it made my skin and eyes itchy as FUUUCCKKK and it also just looked like straight ass so I decided against it for obvious reasons!!! It was for the best because my PB cosplay still looked great without it in my opinion, also I felt very euphoric in that cosplay so that boosted my confidence by like one billion. I felt very #GenderAffirmed and very very much like Paintbrush!! I like doing things that make me feel more connected to my sources and kins it's very very fun in my humble opinion


Part of me wanted to take pictures with other cosplayers but I genuinely HAATE just randomly going up to people I don't know, it's nerve wracking as shit and I'm really not much of a social person so asking to take a picture would've felt like a serious violation of a boundary LMFAO. The most I did was just compliment them and move on and a lot of them also complemented my cosplay so I'm satisfied with just that!! I did push myself a bit more than I usually would to talk to others through giving out stickers that I personally designed myself so that was a fuck ton of fun. I made stickers of the 4 main Bright Lights (Lightbulb, Paintbrush, Test Tube and Fan) and out of all them the majority of people wanted a sticker of Fan who just so happens to be my least favorite of the four LMFAOOOOOO, and yes I did keep count of how many stickers I gave out in total as well as how many of each individual character people asked for. I managed to give out a total of 20 stickers (7 of Fan, 6 of Lightbulb, 4 of Test Tube, and only 3 of Paintbrush), and managed to get a lot of free goodies from other people in the process! Lot's of kandi bracelets and pins as well as a handful of stickers, someone even gave me win tokens that they made from i think cardboard(?) and I thought that was really cute LOL. One interaction I will remember forever was this one person who saw my Paintbrush cosplay and my Lightbulb plushie that I had in my seethrough-backpack and decided that they had to give me a Lightbrush pin #LIGHTBRUSHFROEVERTHANKYOUSOMUCH. It was great. Crazy to me though that the II character I relate to the most and see so much of myself in and also am literally a fucking ficive of and was cosplaying had the least amount of people wanting a sticker of them, although Lightbulb (aka my gorgeous wife and girlfriend) was the second most popular right behind Fan so I can forgive,,,,,,


I pulled up to New York with my dad that very Saturday expecting the venue to be incredibly fucking hectic because mom had called the venue the previous meetup day that weekend to get some insight on what was happening and the guy on the phone with her apparently said something about there being people camping outside the venue for hours so that they could be the first to get merch??? and that they were selling out of the shit really quick So due to that I was very much prepared to Not be able to get merch at all as was trying to not get my hopes up but to my surprise it was actually very fucking calm the morning we went and people were just lined up outside the venue like normal LOL. all that fearmongering from my mom for nothing! I got my filthy sweaty gay hands on a Poppy plush and I like to swing them around and beat them sometimes. I love Poppy btw they're one of my favorite characters I just do this to them because I find it funny and also they give me hella cuteness aggression. But Anyways!


The venue was pretty crowded obviously that's a given with this kind of event and how big the OSC is, but it was not at all a stressful environment to be in and everyone I personally had the pleasure of interacting with was very very kind! I do wish I got to interact with others just a bit more but I was standing on buisness with giving out stickers and did not move from the spot me and my dad claimed right outside the theater doors until it was time for us to go in and find our seats LOL. Regardless though I still am grateful I got to talk to people at all even if it was only for a moment with a majority of them. By far the funniest thing was this one kid who was in the middle of the lobby very loudly singing Taco's Tirade, and then a bunch of other people came around to sing along with him and this one Taco cosplayer started blasting that fuckass BFDI fansong by BenjixScarlett. I had no words I just turned to my dad who was sitting on the bench behind me understanding absolutely None of what was happening and said "the kids are alright". I did have to step away because it was becoming a bit much (all these people were like in a circle right in front of me) but my childlike wonder was stolen from me long ago so y'know who am I to judge others for still having theirs. i've heard a lot of other people talk about their experience at the meetups they went to throughout this past July be filled with a lot of people being very rowdy and just overall not very respectful of the people around them but that genuinely was not the case for the day that I went? at least not for me personally, i really haven't heard anything from people who went on the same day as me so I'm not sure of what other people's experiences were like that particular morning LOL.


I'm not really sure what else to say to be honest,, the new II episode was and still is incredible obviously, waiting a few weeks for it to release officially on Youtube to be able to talk about it wasn't as hard as I thought it would be LMFAO, the QNA was also great too!! Lot's of interesting questions were asked, not a single person asked about ships (thank fucking god) and there were no Unfortunate Incidents that occured (iykyk). My friend who went to the meetup last year ranted on their private twitter a couple days ago about the tour is honestly a bit expensive for how little it has to offer which like. honestly? i agree?? aside from obviously getting to see the episodes several weeks earlier than the rest of the fandom, the QNA, and the meet and greet (if you were rich enough to be able to afford a VIP ticket) there really isn't much to do aside from just talk to other people there before and after the main event, which can be really difficult if you aren't a very social person. i personally had to really push myself in order to feel confident in just giving out stickers to people and it's really only because of that that I was able to socialize just a little bit, but I did still have a lot of fun and this still was an icredibly valuable experience for me. If they continue to do these tours it would be cool if they introduced different activities other than just the screening and QNA. Whatever happens in the future though I would still love to have the opportunity to go again!
