POWERBULB!

Powerbulb is my yume/oc x canon/self-insert/whatever-you-wanna-call-it ship between Lightbulb from Inanimate Insanity and Power Drill, my object oc/sona that i've inserted into the II universe! He is transmasc, pangender, and lesbian. He uses he/xe/it pronouns, but mostly just prefers he/him. His nickname is "Drilly", as given to him by Lightbulb herself, and he enjoys drawing and building things!

Power Drill was a rejected contestant who never got the chance to compete on Inanimate Insanity during it's original runtime, lacking a lot of social skills as well as understanding of himself and his own emotions. He's incredibly awkward and tries a bit too hard to appear friendly to others, and is cursed with a constant fear of rejection and being viewed as "weird" or any other negative thing by the people around him. It wasn't until he escaped MeAfterLife and came across Lightbulb that he felt he was able to let loose and just be himself. The start of their relationship was a bit rocky, with Power Drill being offput by someone finally actually wanting to be around him, instead of treating him like a parasite. As a result he acted in ways towards Lightbulb that were his worst nightmare when interacting with others: avoidant and dismissive. Naturally this was upsetting to Lightbulb, as she was just excited to talk to someone new and didn't mean any harm, but she kept trying with Power Drill until he eventually realized it was fine for him to let the wall down, and they became relatively close with each other.

The more time they spent together, the more Power Drill began to feel things for her, but he didn't exactly fully understand what these feelings were, nor did he want to make Lightbulb uncomfortable, so he just kept quiet about it out of extreme fear of potentially ruining the bond they had. Not to mention she was already in a loving relationship with Paintbrush, so he felt as if he would be intruding on them if he were to act on these feelings. So he simply ignored his feelings and shoved them down, but to his dismay they only became more and more extreme over time, causing him to form a bit of resentment and jealousy towards Paintbrush. Them and Lightbulb were perfect for each other, obviously. The two went together like PB&J. They loved each other more than anyone and anything else on the island, and in the world in general. What chance did Power Drill even have?

He quickly began trying to distance himself from Lightbulb as to not get himself hurt, but he found this inredibly difficult as he had become quite attached to her, being reliant on her very presence to feel happy. He wanted nothing but to spend every second of every day with her, but he knew she had other people in her life she wanted to be around too. Besides, there was practically no way she would ever love him when she already had a joyfriend. So he simply shut himself away again and began avoiding her like he had done in the past, trying to force himself to simply move on. Lightbulb was quick to pick up on this, however, and inquired about what was wrong. After a bit of trying to muster up the courage, Power Drill decided to confess right then and there. To his surprise, Lightbulb admitted that she could tell for awhile that he was into her, and that she felt the same.

Luckily, Paintbrush was open to the idea of being polyamorous. Although they were a bit skeptical of Power Drill considering they had taken notice of some of his questionable behaviors, as well as being pretty attached to Lightbulb themself and all, they didn't mind allowing the two of them to give things a shot. Power Drill and Paintbrush have a bit of a hard time getting used to each other and have a bit of a rivalry at first, but they learned to tolerate each other for Lightbulb's sake as she loves the both of them very much. Eventually PD and PB also catch feelings for each other after spending more time together and PD learns to be more mindful of how he acts and the three of them live happily ever after in the world's most amazing T4T4T dyke polycule ever HURRAAAAAAAAAAAH.
