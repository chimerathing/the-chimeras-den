ARE VOCALSYNTHS AI (ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE)?

Short answer: yes and no! it depends on the software and (in my opinion) there's a lot more nuance when it comes to the ethics of it.

Long answer: vocalsynths started out as something completely devoid of anything remotely related to the usage of AI. Softwares that have been around since the beginning such as VOCALOID (specifically versions 2-5) and UTAU utilize somehting known as "Concatenative Synthesis". The way it works is that a voice provider records a large amount voice samples in which they read out a string of syllables/phonemes at a certain pitch. These samples are later on manually configured into a voicebank, and the raw recordings are directly sampled and distorted based on user input. This method results in a voicebank that can only sing in one language as well as a usually very limited vocabulary depending on the amount of samples that were recorded. If you want your voicebank to sing in more than one language or even be able to switch to a different style of singing, you'd have to record seperate samples for those and repeat the process all over again, and thus would have to make it an entirely seperate voicebank. These kinds of voicebanks also generally sound more choppy and robotic, which is what vocalsynths tend to be known for. All the files for the voicebanks are stored directly on your computer.

As time went on, the technology advanced and eventually AI was introduced into the mix. Softwares such as SynthV, Cevio/Voisona, Diffsinger, and VOCALOID6 (the latest version of VOCALOID) can technically be classified as generative AI softwares, but not in the traditional sense. They don't make use of concatenative synthesis but instead, voicebanks are sampled from recordings of a voice provider singing normally. These recordings are then fed into an algorithm as data to create an AI voice model. Rather than sampling directly from the raw recordings, the AI calculates data and creates a sound based on user input. This method results in a voicebank that can sing in virtually any language with an infinite vocabulary, as well as a voice that sounds EXTREMELY similar to an actual human-being singing. These voicebanks typically also come with multiple singing styles (aka appends/vocal modes) bundled with them by default and they can easily be blended in with eachother, unlike with concatenative voicebanks where if you want a particular character to sing in a different tone than usual, that would have to be made into an entirely seperate voicebank. Almost all of these softwares run locally on your computer rather than from a server or data center, heavily minimizing any possible negative effects on the environment.

Pretty much all of these softwares still majorily require input from the user and you have the option of either manually tuning the voicebank yourself or using the "autotune" feature in which pitch bends are randomly calculated and generated based on a dataset for that specific bank. It's still technically "generating" a result for you based on all of your inputs and settings in your project file. But again, it is NOT a traditional form of generative AI as it is not generating an entire song for you, nor is it generating a random voice based off of stolen work from non-consenting real singers or voice actors. It's meant to be used as an instrument just like vsynths that use concatenative synthesis, and all voice providers are consenting and are paid for their work. For me personally, it's the only form of generative AI that I will ever support, since it's still meant to be used as a creative tool rather than something to entirely replace real human art and creativity. The vocalsynth community rightfully looks down on those who use AI to generate entire songs, because it's a community that embraces genuine creativity, not souless and effortless slop. If you're an genAI lover looking to capitalize of off the vocalsynth community with your garbage that is only able to exist due to the stolen work of real artists, then I personally think you should die. If you want a more in depth explanation about this, I suggest watching JOEZcafe's video on this topic! They explain it way better than I do LMFAO.

I think more people need to acknowledge the reality that some vsynths are unfortunately, in a sense, apart of the generative AI bubble that we all so desperately are waiting on to pop, but they're so far removed from what we typically think of when we see the words "generative AI" that I don't think we should criticize them on the sole basis of them utilizing AI, but rather based on the practices and ethics of the particular companies behind them. Vsynths as a whole are still something that can be considered a genuine form of art and it's not an entirely black and white situation. I think we should focus on the ways more traditional forms generative AI (the kind that just shits out an end result for you with no real effort required) is being used to replace humanity. It steals from artists, makes people stupid and incapable of forming their own thoughts, and is actively poisoning our planet 10 times faster than ever before due to dozens of servers dedicated to a chatbot that's being used to make deepfakes of minorities to incite bigotry against them and to sexualize non-consenting individuals, and on top of that, has lead to the suicide of many that became dependant on a fucking robot controlled by fascists for social interaction. Vocal synths that utilize AI are a special and more nuanced case and I personally don't think that they should be grouped in with generative AI as a whole, but that's just my take. If you think differently than me on this matter, then that's perfectly fine!
