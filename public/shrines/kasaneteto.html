WHO IS KASANE TETO?

Kasane Teto is a vocal synth character with voicebanks available for the softwares UTAU, TALQu, Synthesizer V Studio, and VOICEPEAK. She orignated as a hoax created by users of 2chan (Japan's equivalent to 4chan), also called VIP@2ch, in which they would create a line of characters for the purpose of tricking people into thinking they were the next voicebanks to be released by Crypton Future Media for the VOCALOID software. This group of characters are referred to as VIPPERLoids. Teto herself was the very first VIPPERloid, and a fake demo video of her was released on NicoNicoDouga on April 1st, 2008, which was just a pitched up recording of her voice provider Oyamano Mayo singing "Miku Miku ni Shite Ageru". Teto's first actual voicebank was released April 13th, 2008, for the UTAU software, and was of course voiced by the same person who sung her original demo.

She's regarded as one of the primary reasons the UTAU software became popular due to her infamy as a fake, as well as her general fame as a character. Her popularity ultimately lead to the creation of other VIPPERloids, the most popular aside from Teto being Yokune Ruko, Namine Ritsu, and Sukone Tei. She was also the first UTAU created with samples of a real human's voice, since Uta Utane (also known as Defoko), the very first UTAU voicebank as well as the voicebank that comes preinstalled with the software, had her voice sampled from the machine-synthesized text-to-speech software "AquesTalk".

She went relatively unnoticed by the majority of vocal synth producers for a while until near the end of 2008, when several producers began composing songs for her, officially cementing her place in the hearts of many vocal synth fans. The doujin circle "TWINDRILL" was founded in 2009 for the purpose of managing and promoting the character, and they established a cooperative relationship with Crypton Future Media in 2010, leading to Teto officially appearing in the Project Diva franchise and thus being commonly mistaken as an official VOCALOID by many fans. Her immense popularity among vocal synth fans throughout the years would eventually lead to TWINDRILL's collaboration with AH-Software to create her Synthesizer V AI voicebank, announced on April 1st, 2023 and later officially released on April 27, 2023. Her talk-voicebanks for TALQu and VOICEPEAK were released in 2021 and 2025 respectively.

At some point, a prototype VOCALOID voicebank was created for Teto, but it was never officially released due to it being unable to properly capture what made her UTAU voicebank unique and charming. A famous headcanon within the community was that Teto's biggest dream was to become a VOCALOID, so it's ironic how that ended up never officially happening. This headcanon came from one of her most well-known songs being "Kasane Territory" (which is a parody of SilverForest's "⑨destiny ~ Zutto Cirno no Turn" by oxi) in which she mentions dreaming of becoming a VOCALOID right before the final chorus. While she may not have become what she originally wanted, she did become something far superior, right? Well, at least in my opinion.

She is 31 years old, but she ages at half the speed of normal humans due to being a chimera (that being said, she's 15 in chimera years). Her gender is also described as being chimera, which makes my chimeragender-as-one-of-my-many-xenogenders ass really happy. Who knew violently bigoted 2channers in 2008 were capable of making several queer icons? It's beautiful. Her favorite food is baguettes, and she's described as being rather mischievous in terms of personality, alongside being a bit of a "tsundere".

While TWINDRILL has never released any official statement regarding the meaning of her name, her surname 重音 (Kasane) is interperated by many to literally mean "heavy sound" or "overlapped sound". Her name テト (Teto) is derived from "Tetopettenson", the name of a parody version of the song "Le Beau Tambour".
