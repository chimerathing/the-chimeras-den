MY QUEER LABELS!

I'm a HUMONGOUS label hoarder, these are just the main ones I use that define the most important parts of my queer identity. Friendly reminder that I'm not interested in debating people on the validity of my identity. If something here bothers you, that's an issue you need to work out within yourself. If you're simply confused and would like to be educated, be my guest! Just remember to be respectful of me. My identity is mine and I do not owe anyone any sort of justification for who I am, nor am I obligated to change myself to be palatable for you.


TRANSMASC

a transgender individual who identifies primarily with masculinity/a masculine gender and is thus transitioning/has transitioned to represent that.


DEMIBINARY

when one's gender is partially non-binary and partially binary.


PANGENDER

someone who is countless, infinite, or all genders that are within their culture and life experiences (im primarily male but also butchgender as well as countless neogenders and xenogenders).


XENOGENDER

a nonbinary identity and umbrella term in which ones gender goes beyond the human understandings of gender, and can best be described by other methods of gender categorization. these genders are related to things or concepts that are not traditionally associated with gender and are often used by the neurodivergent community, but the term is not exclusive.


BUTCHBOY

someone who is both a butch and a boy for any reason. a butch is any queer person who is an embodiment of queer masculinity or is masculine in a way thats deliberately subversive and blatantly rejects patriarchal binary gender norms.


BUTCHGENDER

a gender that feels intrinsically tied to one's butch identity / literally is the concept of butchness itself.


LESBOY

a lesbian that's a boy or likes being seen as one for any reason. for me this is due to being multigender and primarily resonating with manhood and masculinity.


ABRO LESBIAN

primarily being lesbian/queerly attracted to exclusively women but occassionally feeling attracted to other genders (you will never catch me being attracted to any cisgendernormative man).


FICTOJECTUM

being both fictosexual/romantic (attracted to fictional characters) and objectum (attracted to objects) and both of those attractions being influenced by one another.


PLUSQUEER

a queer inclusionst label representing the following ideologies:

1. inclusivity of abinary identities, ranging from more common ids (like non-binary) to lesser common ids (like xenogenders). this also includes the support of intersexuality and intersex-specific identities.

2. supportive of neopronouns and gender non-conforming language.

3. inclusivity of all good-faith/contradictory identities, like lesboys/turigirls, gaybians, and bi-lesbians/pan-gays.

4. inclusivity of cultural lgbtq identities like two-spirit.

5. being anti-harassment, anti-harm, and pro-activism; being willing to fight for both ourselves and others in the community.

6. being anti-harmful language, including being against of the use of agab terminology due to it being stolen from intersex folk and used to subtly misgender trans folk and reinforce the gender binary.

7. being supportive of all queer expressions, regardless of if they conform to gender stereotypes or not. this includes those who consider nonhumanity a part of their queer identity.

8. being open to learning about and being understanding of any and all labels as long as they're not genuinely harmful in some way.

9. progressivism and looking forward to positive change in the community.
